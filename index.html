Fostering Curiosity and Lifelong Learning

The Roundglass Learn Labs (RGF Global) is on a mission to ignite curiosity and empower individuals through a hands-on, experimental learning model. Our unique approach focuses on Wholistic Wellbeing by creating immersive, co-owned learning environments that enable people to explore new concepts and build skills for a brighter future, primarily in underserved communities in Punjab, India.

We strive to create a vibrant learning ecosystem for rural growth, encompassing the wellbeing of the individual's mind, creative spirit, and ability to thrive in a changing world.

Our Core Learning Pillars

Curiosity & Critical Thinking

Designing and delivering experiential learning solutions (Learn Labs) that move beyond rote memorization. We focus on fostering cognitive flexibility, problem-solving skills, and a lifelong love for inquiry and exploration through hands-on projects and open-ended challenges.

Skill Development & Future Readiness

Equipping youth and community members with relevant skills for the 21st-century economy. This includes digital literacy training, entrepreneurial aptitude programs, and promoting foundational skills like leadership, teamwork, and communication through structured activities and mentorship.

Wholistic Wellbeing Integration

Integrating physical, emotional, and social wellbeing into the learning process. This includes using mediums like sports (e.g., Sports Centers, 1Girl1Football Program) to nurture discipline and potential, and conducting awareness workshops on health and self-care.

🚀 Learn Labs Impact: At a Glance

The Roundglass Learn Labs program has successfully driven significant impact across rural Punjab by fostering curiosity and empowering thousands of children and community members. Our efforts are structured around three core areas: Empowering Learning Spaces, Cultivating Future Readiness, and Integrating Wholistic Wellbeing. We have established over 500 Learn Labs and integrated Sports Centers which function as vital hubs for experiential, project-based learning. These centres provide daily access to specialized resources, engaging over 10,000 students in immersive programs focused on STEM, arts, and critical thinking. Beyond academic support, our future-readiness initiatives have enabled over 2,500 community members to complete digital literacy and entrepreneurial skill development programs, actively bridging the rural-urban skill divide. Furthermore, we ensure comprehensive growth through wellbeing integration, with over 3,000 adolescent girls participating in the 1Girl1Football Program and hundreds of workshops dedicated to health, hygiene, and mental resilience, all working to foster confidence, teamwork, and overall growth.
